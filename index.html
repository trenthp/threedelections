About the data
  • 2020 and 2024 county results: tonmcg/US_County_Level_Election_Results. Boundaries: U.S. Census Bureau via us-atlas. Land area is computed from each county's real geographic area, not its on-screen size.
  • 2026 is the U.S. House midterm (there's no presidential race), with every district's votes summed by county. These are unofficial counts from The New York Times and the Associated Press, compiled by mike10010100/elections-2026. Some counties are still counting, and counties with an uncontested seat show one-sided margins. Turnout is lower in midterms, so blocks are shorter than in presidential years.
  • Alaska reports results by state house district, which don't line up with boroughs, so Alaska is shown as one uniform statewide result.
  • Connecticut 2024 results are reported by the new planning regions. They're mapped onto the legacy counties with an approximate crosswalk.
  • Alaska and Hawaii are drawn as insets at a different scale, so their volumes aren't comparable with the lower 48.
  • 2016 is omitted because the available county file is a pre-certification snapshot whose totals don't match official results.
  • This is an independent visualization. It isn't affiliated with any campaign, party, news organization or election office.
